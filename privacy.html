Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 6, 2025

Overview

VisionProTeleop Viewer ("we", "our", or "the app") is a web-based tool for viewing VisionPro hand tracking recordings. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Data We Access

When you sign in with Google Drive or Dropbox, we access:

  • Google Drive: Read-only access to files in your VisionProTeleop folder
  • Dropbox: Read-only access to files in your Apps/VisionProTeleop folder

Data Storage

We do NOT store your data on any server. All data processing happens in your browser:

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally in your browser's localStorage
  • Recording files are streamed directly from your cloud storage
  • No data is transmitted to any third-party servers

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third parties. This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages with no backend server.

Cookies

We use localStorage to remember your sign-in state. No tracking cookies are used.

Your Rights

You can revoke access at any time:

  • Google: Google Account Permissions
  • Dropbox: Dropbox Connected Apps

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
